llama.cpp reaches 1.0 and promises version numbers will flag breaking changes to its interfaces

Apps embedding llama.cpp can now pin to a version line instead of patching around silent interface breaks.

llama.cpp and its ggml engine now follow semantic versioning: from v1.0.0, API and ABI (binary-level) incompatibilities arrive only with major-version bumps. The project, 3.5 years old, supports more than 150 model architectures and runs CI on hardware from NVIDIA, AMD, Qualcomm, Arm, IBM and Intel. Pin your embedded builds to 1.x, and treat any 2.0 as a planned migration.
