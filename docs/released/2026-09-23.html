Hugging Face lets you load quantised local models without a wrapper

Local quantised models required a separate inference stack; Transformers now loads them natively on Apple Silicon.

Hugging Face's Transformers library now loads GGUF quantised models (the format powering Ollama, LM Studio, and Jan) directly via from_pretrained, no wrapper needed. On Apple Silicon, it reuses llama.cpp's underlying kernels and benchmarks within a few percent of llama.cpp's speed. Load a Q4_K_M checkpoint and drop it into your existing Transformers pipeline today.
