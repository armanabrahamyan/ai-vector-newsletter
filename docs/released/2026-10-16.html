The Pulse · Story of the day

Meta's largest open model reads code, images, audio, and video at once

Open-weight frontier coding agents required proprietary APIs; Meta's 816B model puts that capability on your own hardware.

Meta's Muse Spark Open is an 816B-parameter mixture-of-experts model that activates only 42B parameters per token, built for agentic coding across text, images, audio, and video. Weights and fine-tuning recipes are public on Hugging Face. It needs 32 H200 GPUs in BF16, setting the hardware floor for self-hosted frontier coding agents.

Financial agent safety depends on where controls sit, not how many

Financial firms treated agent safety as a restriction problem; placement of controls is the real variable.

At AI Engineer New York, OpenAI, Anthropic, Two Sigma, and Bloomberg converged on one principle: agent safety in finance is a placement problem, not a restriction count. Two Sigma runs five composable controls, each doing one job, routing only the highest-stakes decisions to humans. The question for your deployment: where does your approval layer sit, and who designed it?

AI voice fraud cost Italy's biggest bank €95 million

Executive-impersonation fraud moved from theoretical threat to a €95 million realised loss at a named European bank.

Fraudsters impersonating Fideuram executives used AI-generated voice or messaging to steal €95 million from Intesa Sanpaolo's private banking arm, two anonymous sources told Reuters. No systems breach; the attack vector was identity, not infrastructure. If your payment-authorisation chain relies on voice confirmation from senior leaders, who in your org can actually verify the caller is real?

Nubank doubled training throughput by fixing attention kernels and compiler setup

Transformer training budgets assumed kernel defaults were optimal; Nubank's production run proves they rarely are.

Nubank's engineering blog details how switching to cuDNN attention kernels and fixing compiler graph breaks lifted training efficiency from 21% to 39% on a hybrid architecture. No single architectural change drove the gain; kernel selection alone added 7 percentage points. Audit your own training stack's kernel selection before your next experiment cycle.

Three preprints and one infrastructure release share a common direction: deployment constraints that previously required human judgment or bespoke configuration are being absorbed into the model or platform layer itself. Each item sits here because the evidence is single-source and unreplicated, not because the trajectory is unclear.

A pre-trained model that resizes itself to fit any hardware budget

Inference sizing required committing to a fixed model family; one elastic checkpoint now spans the full compute range.

Fixed model sizes force a hardware commitment at deployment. An arXiv preprint converts any pre-trained transformer into one elastic checkpoint that trims neurons and attention heads on demand, reaching 1.8× throughput at 60% sparsity with under 1% accuracy loss. The code is public and the published benchmark covers accuracy and throughput across five sparsity levels.

A reinforcement-learning system reads finance papers to build trading signals

Quant factor discovery required human researchers; a trained language model now reads the literature directly.

AlphaRLM, an arXiv preprint, trains a language model via reinforcement learning to read financial research papers and construct executable trading factors without seed expressions. Tested on CSI 300, CSI 1000, and S&P 500, the 8-billion-parameter model beats established baselines on annualised excess return. The cross-market results remain preliminary findings in a preprint, not a validated deployment framework.

Databricks cuts the networking tax on serverless data workloads

Serverless networking required per-resource proxy deployments; one delegated subnet now covers everything.

More than 75% of Databricks customers cited network complexity as the reason workloads stayed on classic compute. Public preview on Azure; AWS in private preview. Public preview on Azure makes the gateway available to test today.
